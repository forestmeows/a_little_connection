Approved scope · Working title · Ages 18–25

Youth AI Connection Companion

Notice possible reliance on AI. Encourage balanced advice. Open a path to a real human conversation.

A working demo chat with an extension-style panel. Scope approved by the learner.

The distinctive idea

Combine transparent chatbot response guidance with an optional real-person handoff. The human connection is central to the proof.

Who it serves

Young people seeking emotional or moral advice who may increasingly prefer AI over people they know. A nonprofit youth-volunteer service is the longer-term vision; no partnership is established.

The journey we will demonstrate

This flow connects the intended user, the core interaction, and the evidence that the handoff works.

  1. 1 user, demo chat

    A fictional exchange includes repeated preference for AI over friends.

  2. Companion checks in

    A gentle prompt offers connection. Disclosed guidance shapes the next chatbot reply.

  3. User chooses connection

    The user requests a volunteer conversation. Interest groups are also available to browse.

  4. A real person replies

    A consenting adult uses a separate volunteer view. Their reply appears in the user's chat.

The proof of success

In a short screen demo, show the pattern, check-in, notice, balanced chatbot reply, and a message sent by a second human and received by the user. Then browse sample communities.

This demonstrates a working interaction and handoff. It does not establish a diagnosis of dependency, detection accuracy, or improved wellbeing. Ordinary appreciation or empathy alone is insufficient evidence.

Now — one complete demo journey
  • Our own demo chat and an extension-style companion panel.
  • Defined pattern detection and a gentle, dismissible check-in.
  • Balanced-response guidance with a visible notice.
  • Working user-to-volunteer text conversation using two views and a real second person.
  • Browsable sample communities; membership is not required.
  • Fictional exchanges and consenting adult role-players.
  • Planning docs, public code, and a short demo video. Deployment is optional.
Later — integrations and live communities
  • Browser extension integration with existing chatbot websites, after investigating feasibility.
  • Community membership, optional anonymity, and group conversations. Group chat is a stretch goal after the core works.
  • Nonprofit partnership, staffing, and age-appropriate safeguarding, privacy, and moderation before a real youth-service launch.
  • Evaluation of detection quality and actual benefits.
Cut from this proof — with reasons
  • Production service for minors: operational arrangements are not established.
  • Claims of diagnosis or improved wellbeing: the demo cannot substantiate them.
  • Automatic changes to third-party chatbot backends: the first version uses our own chat so the interaction can work without unverified access.

Why this project

The learner wants to address reliance on AI for emotional support by increasing connections with real people. Their immediate learning goal is to get a working product.

Next: product requirements

Define exact signals, user choices, consent for sharing with a volunteer, volunteer availability, response limits, and the visual style. These details are still open.
