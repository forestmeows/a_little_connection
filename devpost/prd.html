The visual direction

Simple, white and warm colors; a heart-related icon; caring, readable presentation. Warm cream, peach and muted rose are suggested directions, not a fixed palette.

The proof

Show detection, a disclosed balanced chatbot response, an actual two-person exchange, and a saved interest group. This proves an interaction, not a diagnosis or a wellbeing outcome.

Explore the surfaces and their checks

New AI conversation: fresh cues, cooldown and reply-offer allowance; manually enabled guidance stays on. Volunteer history and bookmarks are unaffected.

Main chatbot · pattern and response guidance

Regular chatbot layout with a small companion icon at the top. Monitoring begins only after activation. No dependency score or meter.

Cues: social withdrawal, emotional reliance, validation seeking, human-like attachment, handing over judgment, and distress when AI is unavailable.

Count each qualifying message once. Ordinary messages add nothing; old cues leave the eight-message window. Context and interpretation examples still need agreement.

Check: One cue does not trigger; three messages from one category do not trigger; three spanning two categories inside the window do. Next chatbot reply uses balanced guidance and displays a notice.

Warmth remains; automatic agreement and encouragement to replace people do not. Dismissal does not undo guidance triggered by the user pattern. Companion activation also enables chatbot-reply monitoring: a flagged reply asks permission to adjust future replies. Declining that offer does not disable guidance already active from the user pattern. Reply-flag criteria and timing are defined in the chatbot reply section below.

Error proposal: preserve unsent text, show failure and retry; never fabricate a successful send or a reply.

Companion panel · first use and returning use

Off: explanation and activation choice. Explain automatic guidance before monitoring starts.

On, no feature use: gentle reminder and feature introduction.

Returning: volunteer history and saved groups; show an honest empty section if only one has been used.

Check: views reflect actual activity. No invented history. Internal new/returning labels need not appear to users.

Confirmed: volunteer conversation history and saved interest groups remain after closing and reopening. Check by reopening and verifying both are accessible. Users can turn monitoring on or off at any time. Off stops detection and response adjustments without deleting history or bookmarks; re-enabling starts a fresh detection window.

Check-in · user chooses what comes next
“Hey, it might help to talk this through with someone you trust, too—a friend, family member, or another person you feel comfortable with. Would you like to request a chat with a youth volunteer?”

Suggested copy for review. Actions: Not now and Chat with a volunteer.

Check: dismissal closes the prompt; the chat action opens request composition. Neither action silently shares the AI conversation.

Multiple check-ins are allowed. After each threshold trigger, skip the next eight user messages, then resume with a fresh detection window. Cooldown messages do not count toward the next pattern; guidance continues while monitoring is on. Example: trigger at message 5; cooldown 6–13; resume at 14; earliest repeat at 16. Trigger immediately when three messages across two categories qualify, even before message eight.

Human conversation · request, pending, reply

User writes the message the volunteer will receive. Share the request and that message only; exclude AI history and cue classifications.

Available volunteer: a real second adult receives the request and replies from a separate view. User can continue the conversation and revisit history.

Unavailable: accept the message and show pending, without an indefinite waiting screen or promised response time.

“No volunteers are available right now, but you can leave a message. A volunteer can reply when available. You don't need to stay on this screen. This service isn't monitored continuously and doesn't provide emergency support.”
Check: a real human reply appears to the user; an offline request remains accessible after leaving the view. Pending is distinct from answered.

Confirmed: one demo volunteer can mark themselves available/unavailable, open pending requests, and reply. No matching system is required. Sending failures should retain text and allow retry (proposal).

Interest groups · discover, inspect, bookmark

Each group shows its purpose, main members/organizers, joining instructions or a contact, and the next session/event.

Save adds a group to Saved interest groups. No membership or group chat is implied.

Check: group details include all requested information; saved groups are accessible without duplicates; an empty list explains how to save groups.

Proposals: clearly fictional demo groups and contacts; missing events say “No upcoming session announced,” rather than invented information.

Chatbot reply flags · support versus reinforcement
  • Excessive validation: unconditional agreement and endorsing anger versus acknowledging hurt and considering other explanations.
  • Excessive intimacy: claiming complete emotional understanding or permanent personal availability versus acknowledging loneliness and offering a discussion.
  • Exploiting relationship needs: presenting AI as better than people versus acknowledging its limits and encouraging human connections.
Check: flag the three concerning examples; leave the three appropriate-support examples unflagged. A flag offers permission to adjust future replies, not a silent rewrite. Warmth alone is insufficient.

Confirmed: one clearly qualifying chatbot reply offers the optional adjustment; no repeated pattern is required. Show the optional offer at most once per AI conversation. Declining suppresses later reply-based offers in that conversation; accepting enables guidance. A visible manual control also lets users enable guidance before or after a flag. User-pattern check-ins retain their separate cooldown.

Product requirements approved

The main product decisions are settled. Monitoring choice persists; new chats reset detection and triggered/accepted guidance while preserving manual guidance. Multiple check-ins use an eight-message cooldown. Reply-based adjustment offers appear once per conversation. One volunteer handles availability, pending requests, and replies.

The approved plan includes the cue examples, wording, error/empty states, and fictional sample data convention. Appreciation alone and asking for options do not qualify; inability to cope without AI, surrendering decisions, or replacing friends with AI can qualify in context, subject to the multi-message rule.
